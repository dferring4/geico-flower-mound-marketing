# GEICO Flower Mound marketing assets

Public, approved marketing artwork and reusable email templates for GEICO Flower Mound. Do not add customer information, passwords, unpublished policy information, or unapproved brand artwork.

## Email image hosting

Upload `geico_bundle_banner.png` to `images/` on the `main` branch. The stable, publicly accessible image address for the email template will be:

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dferring4/geico-flower-mound-marketing/main/images/geico_bundle_banner.png`

Open that address in an incognito window to verify the actual image is displayed before sending an email. The image must exist on `main` first; merely linking a proposed URL does not publish the image.

## Outlook

The email template should use table-based, inline CSS and absolute HTTPS image URLs, with a text alternative for blocked images. Recipients' Outlook settings may block remote images by default. Test by sending the finished email from Classic Outlook to desktop and mobile inboxes. Discounts depend on eligibility; do not guarantee a specific discount or effective date.

Customer email sends must use your office's approved compliance, unsubscribe, and suppression process.
